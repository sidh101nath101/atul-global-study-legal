Privacy Policy — Atul Global Study

App: Atul Global Study (package atul.global.study)
Effective date: 31 October 2026
Developer: Neha Bharti
Contact: nehabharti@niharikaonlinelearning.in

Summary

Atul Global Study is a study app for Bihar exams. To create your student account we collect your name, mobile number and email ID. We do not sell your data, show advertisements, or use analytics or tracking tools. Lessons and mock tests are stored inside the app.

Information we collect

  • Name, mobile number and email ID — entered by you at sign-up. Used to create and secure your account (your email ID receives a one-time login code) and to contact you about your account.
  • Purchase status — whether you own “Full Access”. The payment itself is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

Information stored only on your phone

Your language choice, text size, reading progress, the topics you have opened and your mock-test answers are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. They are erased when you delete your account in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.

Where your account details are kept

Your name, mobile number and email ID are stored with our login service provider, Supabase (database and email-code login), and the login email is sent through our email provider Hostinger. These providers process data only on our behalf, to run the login. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Text to speech

The “Listen” feature uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. The app does not send lesson text to us.

Your choices — deleting your data

In the app, open My account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and the name, mobile number and email ID stored with our login service. You can also request deletion without the app by emailing nehabharti@niharikaonlinelearning.in from your registered email ID; we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play purchase; manage purchases and refunds in Google Play.

Retention

We keep your account details until you delete your account.

Children

The app is intended for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

Security

Account details are stored with access rules so that a student can see only their own record, and are transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.
